FEATURES

Hand-animated effects

Every flame, bolt, splash and shatter is a frame-by-frame pixel effect, not a particle blur.

Three heroes, eight weapons

The Knight swings any of eight weapons. The Ranger fires arrows across the arena, and the Arcane Mage throws bolts from a crystal staff.

Ten bosses

Readable telegraphs: a ring on the floor fills up, then the strike lands. Each one gets angrier at half health, and each drops a treasure chest.

Eleven spells

Five spells unlock as you play, and each has an alternate to buy: Ice Lance, Arcane Beam, Black Hole, Holy Pillar and Divine Shield.

Stars and upgrades

Three stars per stage for clearing, staying healthy and beating par time. Spend coins on skills, health and new weapons.

Sword techniques

Chain three hits into a finisher, attack mid-dodge for a dash strike, or hold attack for a charged uppercut.

Endless mode

Waves until you fall in any arena you've reached, with a boss every fifth wave. How far can you get?

Remix worlds

Earn stars to open remixes: blue frostfire, a blighted crypt, a golden hollow, a blood moon and the abyss.

Made for phones

Multi-touch controls, any screen shape from 4:3 tablets to 21:9 phones, notch-safe HUD and adjustable button size.

SEVEN WORLDS

Three stages each, the third ends in a boss. Every world brings its own enemies and hazards.

World 1

Ashen Crypt

Torch-lit halls full of slimes, bats and ghosts. A gentle start, then the King Slime leaps for you.

Boss: King Slime

World 2

Frostbite Cavern

Icicles drip, tremble and drop. Frost slimes and ghosts guard the cave of the Frost Wraith.

Boss: Frost Wraith

World 3

Thornwood

Poison clouds hang over the forest floor. Frogs, snakes and birds, and the Elder Toad in the old pond.

Boss: Elder Toad

World 4

Ember Forge

Flame vents erupt from the grating. Magma slimes burst, wisps dive, and the Inferno Skull rains fire.

Boss: Inferno Skull

World 5

Void Rift

Shadow hands reach up through the floor while lightning storms roll. At the end waits the Eye of the Void.

Boss: Eye of the Void

World 6

Sunken Sanctum

A drowned temple of geysers and crashing waves. Jellyfish drift, mages cast from range, and the Tide Matriarch rises.

Boss: Tide Matriarch

World 7

Coral Deep

The ocean floor, where the current pushes everyone one way, then back. Pufferfish puff up spiky, crabs guard with a claw, and the Kraken waits in its lair.

Boss: The Kraken

REMIX WORLDS

Earn 6 stars in a world to open its remix: the same arena in new colours, with rules of its own.

THE ARSENAL

Buy weapons in the shop's Armory and equip one. Your attack button becomes that weapon.

SPELLS

Unlocked by clearing stages, upgraded with coins: +30% damage and −15% cooldown per level. Each one has an alternate you can buy and swap in.

Fireball

Flies straight and sets enemies burning. Melts frozen ones.

Lightning

A sigil marks the nearest enemy, then a bolt stuns everything near it.

Frost Nova

The knight slams the ground; everything close freezes solid.

Meteor

A burning rock comes down on a whole group.

Heal

A ring of light restores your health over a few seconds.

Ice Lance

Fireball's alternate: pierces every enemy in a line and freezes them.

Arcane Beam

Lightning's alternate: a beam from the hand that hits everything in front three times.

Black Hole

Frost Nova's alternate: drags enemies in for two seconds, then crushes them.

Holy Pillar

Meteor's alternate: a column of light that stuns everything it touches.

Divine Shield

Heal's alternate: four seconds where nothing can hurt you.
