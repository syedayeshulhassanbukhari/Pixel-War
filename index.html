FEATURES

Hand-animated effects

Every flame, bolt, splash and shatter is a frame-by-frame pixel effect from the pixelfx library, not a particle blur.

Eight weapons

Sword, daggers, claws, spear, whip, hammer, greatsword and scythe, each with its own swing, reach and hit timing.

Boss fights

Five bosses with readable telegraphs: a ring on the floor fills up, then the strike lands. Watch out at half health, when they get angry.

Six skills

Fireball, lightning, frost nova, meteor and heal unlock as you clear stages. Fire melts ice; frozen enemies shatter.

Stars and upgrades

Three stars per stage for clearing, staying healthy and beating par time. Spend coins on skills, health and new weapons.

Made for phones

Multi-touch controls, any screen shape from 4:3 tablets to 21:9 phones, notch-safe HUD and adjustable button size.

FIVE WORLDS

Three stages each, the third ends in a boss. Every world brings its own enemies and hazards.

World 1

Ashen Crypt

Torch-lit halls full of slimes, bats and ghosts. A gentle start, then the King Slime leaps for you.

Boss: King Slime

World 2

Frostbite Cavern

Icicles drip, tremble and drop. Frost slimes and ghosts guard the cave of the Frost Wraith.

Boss: Frost Wraith

World 3

Thornwood

Poison clouds hang over the forest floor. Frogs, snakes and birds, and the Elder Toad in the old pond.

Boss: Elder Toad

World 4

Ember Forge

Flame vents erupt from the grating. Magma slimes burst, wisps dive, and the Inferno Skull rains fire.

Boss: Inferno Skull

World 5

Void Rift

Shadow hands reach up through the floor while lightning storms roll. At the end waits the Eye of the Void.

Boss: Eye of the Void

THE ARSENAL

Buy weapons in the shop's Armory and equip one. Your attack button becomes that weapon.

SKILLS

Unlocked by clearing stages, upgraded with coins: +30% damage and −15% cooldown per level.

Fireball

Flies straight and sets enemies burning. Melts frozen ones.

Lightning

A sigil marks the nearest enemy, then a bolt stuns everything near it.

Frost Nova

The knight slams the ground; everything close freezes solid.

Meteor

A burning rock comes down on a whole group.

Heal

A ring of light restores your health over a few seconds.
